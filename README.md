# HOA Internet and Wi-Fi Quote Checklist

Use this checklist to compare internet proposals for your HOA or condo association.

- [ ] Confirm which homes are covered and how many the provider will bill.
- [ ] Compare installation charges, monthly costs, and renewal terms.
- [ ] Ask where Wi-Fi coverage is included, such as homes, the clubhouse, or outdoor areas.
- [ ] Check whether residents can keep another provider or buy upgrades.
- [ ] Confirm who handles resident support and who owns the equipment.

[Read Crovely’s HOA internet guide](https://crovely.com/insights/bulk-internet-vs-managed-wifi-for-hoas/) for a side-by-side comparison and a billing example.
